# Service card visuals

Original illustrative photographs generated with the built-in image generation tool for this portfolio. These depict service concepts, not actual client projects or team members.

Shared direction: vertical 3:4 editorial photograph, believable workspace, natural light, restrained styling, subject in upper two thirds, no added headings, logos or watermark. Titles and dark overlays are rendered in HTML/CSS.

- `website.png`: A desktop monitor on a natural wooden desk showing a tasteful business website layout, keyboard and notebook, three-quarter camera view.
- `full-stack.png`: A developer workstation with two monitors, one with small code-like colored lines and one with an application dashboard, realistic office setting.
- `automation.png`: A laptop showing a simple workflow automation interface with connected task cards, notebook on desk, warm natural light.
- `voice-agent.png`: A modern telephone headset beside a laptop showing an audio waveform and call interface, natural office photo.
- `api.png`: A developer's hands at a laptop, screen showing structured code and integration interface, understated teal lighting, realistic workspace.
- `ui-ux.png`: Overhead view of a designer's desk with paper wireframes, pencil, and tablet showing mobile interface layouts.
- `landing-page.png`: Laptop and smartphone on a desk displaying a coordinated simple landing page with a single prominent button and large photograph.
- `business.png`: Small business owner seen from behind reviewing a company website on a desktop monitor in a bright modest office.
